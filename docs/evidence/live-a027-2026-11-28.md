## Deviations from the plan, declared

The protocol of S3'' (hypotheses, steady state, injection, reads, window,
pass rule) was run as written. The session's **clock** was not:

1. **About 20 minutes were lost in GitOps (finding 1).** The wait command the
   agent gave for sections 3-4 required every application to be Healthy,
   including `alloy` and `monitoring`. Both need the Grafana secret of
   section 4, the step that comes after the wait, so the loop ran from
   11:00Z to 11:29Z without ending. Everything else was Healthy at 11:05:38Z.
2. **Michele moved the session stops at 11:53Z.** The table printed at apply
   had `terminate-instances` by 11:56Z and the teardown start at 12:16Z. At
   11:53Z Karpenter was at 0 and the page had not yet arrived. Michele chose
   to go on with new stops, `terminate-instances` by 12:05Z and teardown by
   12:20Z, keeping the 12:46Z limit of the budget. `terminate-instances` came
   at 12:03:33Z, inside the new stop and 7 min 33 s after the old one. The
   teardown started at 12:19Z, and the limit held: TEARDOWN CLEAN at about
   12:39Z. The steady state, the wait for the page and the wait for the
   ticket were not shortened to fit.
3. **Teardown:** the command also scaled Karpenter back to 2 before deleting
   the NodePools, so that Karpenter could remove its own nodes. After the
   restore it was already running, so this step changed nothing.

## Findings

1. **The runbook's GitOps wait could never end before the Grafana secret.**
   Section 3 of `live.md` said to wait until every application was Healthy,
   but `alloy` and `monitoring` read the secret of section 4. Fixed in
   this change: section 3 now excludes those two applications and says to
   put the secret as soon as `external-secrets` is Healthy.
2. **The page and the ticket came about 1 minute later than written.** The
   page arrived 8 to 9 minutes after Karpenter went to 0, against about 7
   expected. The ticket arrived 7.5 to 8.5 minutes after the pod left,
   against 5 to 7 expected. S3' had 7 and 8. These times are email times at
   minute resolution, so they include Grafana's evaluation interval, the
   pending period and delivery. Next time, write the hypothesis as 5 to 9
   minutes, or read the alert's firing time from Grafana's state history
   instead of the email.
3. **The A-026 fix to `verify-teardown.sh` worked as intended.** Before the
   destroy it reported the VPC, the CNI ENIs and the cluster SG as LEFT.
   After the destroy all three were 0, so TEARDOWN CLEAN now also means the
   VPC is gone.
