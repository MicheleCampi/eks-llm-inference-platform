Reading, as fixed before the session:

- **E1 and E2 hold**: the kind result holds on EKS across zones. The claim is
  "llm-d-router v1.0.0, default configuration, 2 vLLM CPU v0.34.0 replicas on
  m7i-flex.large (Xeon 8488C) in two zones: prefix-cache hit 0.52 to 0.84,
  TTFT p50 1.43 to 0.43 s", never without the versions and the CPU. It is a
  CPU prefill gain with a 0.5B model and 8 output tokens; no GPU number
  follows.
- The gain is larger than on kind (TTFT -70 % against -39 to -61 %). The
  likely reason, not tested: on EKS each replica has a node of its own, so a
  cache miss costs a full prefill on a 2 vCPU node, while on kind the replicas
  shared one 4-core node.
- **E3 holds**: to be posted on llm-d-router #2059 as real-server parity on
  separate nodes, next to the kind and simulator numbers (draft for Michele,
  not posted).
- On every repetition the `prefix` arm (v1.0.0 default scorers) had a higher
  hit rate than `legacy` and `ea` (main at 32a075e2 with the values of the
  parity test): 0.844 against 0.747 and 0.762. The arms differ in release and
  configuration, so this is not a regression claim; it is a question for the
  #2059 parity config.
- **E5 fails on one request**: a 503 with Envoy's "connection termination"
  text on EKS, with a real network between proxy and engine, 1 in 1920. The
  race of [KEEPALIVE.md](../../experiments/prefix-routing/KEEPALIVE.md) is not a
  kind artefact. Envoy access-log line: see "E5 detail" below.

## Findings

1. **The forced destroy is not automatic, and the time limit was missed by
   about 1 hour.** Limit 19:34Z; destroy started about 20:19Z and completed
   about 20:35Z. Causes: the SSH drop (finding 2) cost 18 minutes; the
   stop condition "goal 2 not finished at apply + 1 h 30 min" (18:34Z) was
   not applied, by the choice of keeping 3 repetitions; and nothing reminded
   the operator at the limit. Estimated extra cost about 0.4 USD, still under
   the ceiling. Fixed in the runbook (limit written down at apply, clock
   checked before every step); the enterprise fix, an automatic destroy at
   the session's time limit, is proposed separately.
2. **A dropped SSH session stopped the foreground loop** at 18:46Z, during
   `service` rep 3. Fixed: the runbook runs `run.sh all` under `nohup` with a
   log file, and `all` is now resumable (runs with a summary are kept).
3. **The chart of llm-d-router main needs `helm dependency build`**: it
   depends on `../routerlib` by `file://`, and `helm upgrade` stopped at the
   first `legacy` run (18:24Z). Fixed in `run.sh`
   (`helm dependency build --skip-refresh` before the install). CI did not
   catch it because `ci/validate-gitops.sh` runs `helm dependency build`
   itself before rendering the arms.
4. **Envoy's 503 "connection termination" appears on EKS** (E5), 1 in 1920.
   Next: the idle-gap test on kind (planned, zero cost), then a proposal to
   the llm-d chart (an upstream idle timeout below the engines' keep-alive,
   or a retry on a reset before any byte), as written in EKS.md.
5. **Apply plan 66 resources, not 67** as in A-021/A-022: to be explained
   from the v2.4.2 diff before the next live.
