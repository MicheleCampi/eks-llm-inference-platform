## Hypotheses

| # | Hypothesis | Result |
|---|---|---|
| K1 | 1 s: more than 1 failure in 960, every failure with flag `UC` | **not met**: 1 failure in the first run, 0 in the second (1 in 1920). The one failure carries flag `UC`. |
| K2 | 600 s: 0 failures in 960 | **met**, in all three runs (0 in 2880) |

As written before the run, with K1 not met the idle-connection race is not
shown by this run.

## Reading

- What the run shows: the failure is Envoy's `UC` flag, "upstream connection
  termination", on the connection from Envoy to the vLLM pod the EPP chose
  (`upstream` is a model-server address). The model server's side of that
  connection closed before any response bytes. The EPP had already done its
  part; the failure happens after routing.
- The rate does not separate the conditions: 1 in 1280 at the vLLM default
  of 5 s (scorer-parity run, both arms), 1 in 1920 at 1 s, 0 in 2880 at 600 s,
  and 1 in 2880 at 5 s on llm-d-router v1.0.0 with the legacy scorers
  (prefix-routing run, 2026-11-23). The failures appear under both scorer
  configurations.
- A limit of the design, found after the run: at concurrency 4 with
  back-to-back requests, a pooled connection is rarely idle for 1 s, so the
  1 s condition did not produce many more idle closes than 5 s. A test of the
  race needs idle gaps between requests (for example pauses of 1 to 5 s);
  this run does not have them.

## Consequence for llm-d-router #2059

The failure in the scorer-parity run is a connection reset between Envoy and
vLLM, recorded with flag `UC` after the EPP had picked the pod, and seen with
the legacy scorers as well. It does not bear on the scorer comparison.
Whether it is the idle-connection race stays open.
