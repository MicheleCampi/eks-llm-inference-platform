## Findings, each to be fixed as code

1. **A false page, firing for the whole session.**
   - The rule: `KarpenterNodePoolCpuLimitReached` (severity page) is `usage >= limit`.
   - The cause: the gated `gpu` pool has a CPU limit of 0 and uses 0, so the rule is always true.
   - When it fired: at 21:04, about 5 min after the metrics arrived. It fired again at 22:08, after Karpenter came back. It "resolved" at 21:44 only because Karpenter's series vanished with the controller at 0.
   - Why it matters: a page that always fires teaches people to ignore pages.
   - Fix: exclude pools whose limit is 0, with a promtool test.
2. **The model is not confined to the `general` pool.**
   - In S3 the replacement replica ran on an On-Demand node of the system node group.
   - That saved the SLO by accident, using capacity sized for the platform.
   - Fix: decided in the [S3 postmortem](../gameday/postmortem-2026-11-20-s3.md).
3. **Both replicas ended in one zone after S1.**
   - The cause was the predicted unavailable-offering rule (1a was blocked for 3 min).
   - Nothing moved them back afterwards. One zone failure would now take both.
   - Fix: a zone topology spread constraint on the model (operator), or Karpenter's spread, plus consolidation.
4. **The first request to a fresh replica takes 17 to 19 s, although the pod is Ready after the Eager warmup.**
   - It cost the latency SLI 1 request in S1 and 2 in S3.
   - The warmup is either not exercising the generation path the probe uses, or the cost is per connection.
   - To investigate in the operator.
   - Follow-up (2026-11-21): neither. `warmupStrategy: Eager` only meant `--enforce-eager`, and readiness on `/health` turned true before any completion had run. Measured in operator CI on the same image and model: 15.9 s cold, 2.0 s after one 1-token completion. Fixed in operator v0.16.0 (a startup probe sends that completion before Ready, CI asserts the first request after Ready within 8 s: 1.88 s), shipped in eks v2.3.8.
5. **Process: in S2 the diagnosis step of `docs/runbooks/workloads.md` was skipped.**
   - The rollback was decided from the watch lines, where `OOMKilled` was visible.
   - The outcome was correct, but the runbook's order was not followed.
6. **Process: a silent wait loop lost about 28 minutes of a paid session.**
   - The operator's SSH dropped on idle at about 20:21 and was reconnected at 20:49.
   - Every wait in a runbook now prints a line per iteration.
7. **Open question.** At 22:01:48, after the restore, Karpenter launched a new `general` node with no Pending model pod. The cause is still to be read in the Karpenter logs of a future session.

## Not proven here

- GPU serving and autoscaling under load. This model is a functional CPU service on 2 vCPU nodes.
- A paged on-call rotation. The pages went to one person, during a planned exercise.
